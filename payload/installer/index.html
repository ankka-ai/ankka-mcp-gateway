Your team’s MCP gateway.

Requires an active domain, Cloudflare Zero Trust, and an account administrator who can create one API token during setup.

How permissions work

Approval 1: Ankka installs the initial gateway, reads your domains, and registers a Workers subdomain if needed. It then revokes the temporary permission.

Approval 2: Your gateway asks permission to finish the configuration you review. Installer grants are revoked.

Management token: Adding sources and managing Team access write to your Cloudflare account long after these approvals are gone, so your gateway needs one account API token of its own. Setup asks for it and links to Cloudflare with both permissions filled in. You paste it into your own gateway; it never passes through Ankka.

Privacy & data

This installer keeps the initial deployment and available domain list for one hour, plus short-lived approval material for your gateway’s one-time key. Your Worker sends its configuration here to certify the callback address. It stores no Cloudflare token and sends no analytics.
