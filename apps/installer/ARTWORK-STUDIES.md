# Local artwork studies

Original, procedural Canvas 2D experiments for the installer preview. Artist names identify research references, not authorship or endorsement. No artwork, source code, or external assets were transferred. These are quiet monochrome adaptations; the source works often use sound, scale, color, physical materials, or data that this preview does not reproduce.

## Kurokawa: four extended studies

These are original synthetic 2D compositions informed by Kurokawa's handling of material and time. They do not use his scans, images, audio, or code. The source works' sound, physical scale, and transitions between recorded environments and reconstructed forms are not reproduced here.

| Study | Composition and motion | Research reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fault | Two oblique granular masses contain fine ridges and voids. Persistent horizontal fragments slip out of registration, remain suspended, and settle. | [subassemblies](https://www.ryoichikurokawa.com/project/sa.html): reconstruction and the relationship between order and disorder. |
| Assemblage | Orthogonal traces and organic patches occupy one shared surface. Sections separate at different depths in the rhythm, then return to registration. | [re-assembli](https://www.ryoichikurokawa.com/project/ra.html): natural and constructed material treated as modules. |
| Fold | A broad, veined granular membrane compresses into a narrow band, pauses, and opens again. | [unfold](https://www.ryoichikurokawa.com/project/unfold.html): matter evolving through formation and collapse; this study uses no astronomical data. |
| Quintet | Five vertical territories answer one another at unequal delays. Shared material, different timing, and moments of collective rest give the field a musical structure. | [rheo: 5 horizons](https://www.ryoichikurokawa.com/project/5horizons.html): five independent audiovisual units forming an ensemble. |

The original motion score repeats a 38-second establish/displace/suspend/re-form phrase, offset across fragments. A new selection starts near the first gesture. Geometry remains stable between frames; variation comes from coordinated transforms. The fine detail remains across the viewport, including behind the text. Grayscale luminance and event frequency control distraction without a central veil. Reduced-motion preference produces a still composition; hidden tabs suspend animation. Sampling is reused across studies, static fragment paths are cached, and resize rebuilding is debounced.
